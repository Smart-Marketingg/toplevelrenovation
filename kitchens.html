Kitchens

Designed around how you live. Built to last.

A kitchen has to look beautiful and work hard every day. We start with how you cook, gather and store things, then plan the layout, cabinetry, counters, lighting and appliances around that, whether you love a clean modern look or warm wood and classic details.

Because we're a full-house remodeler, the kitchen never stops at the cabinets. Moving a wall, opening to the family room, new flooring through the first floor, plumbing and electrical: one team handles all of it on one schedule.

FAQ

Kitchens questions

Something else? Call (732) 538-9379. You'll get a real person, Monday to Friday, 8 to 5.

How much does a kitchen remodel cost?

It depends on the size of the room, layout changes and the finishes you choose, so we give you a free written estimate after an in-home consult. Tell us your budget range in the form below and we'll design to it.

Can you open up a wall between the kitchen and family room?

Often, yes. We'll look at what's in the wall during the consult and plan the framing, electrical and any permits the change needs.

Do you help choose cabinets and finishes?

Yes. Our in-house design team helps with layout, cabinetry, counters, tile, lighting and hardware so everything is chosen before demo day.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Top Level Renovation, Inc. is fully licensed and insured in New Jersey, Home Improvement Contractor #13VH08577300, and BBB Accredited.

Who will I actually deal with during the project?

One dedicated project manager coordinates every trade and every inspection and keeps you posted, usually by group text with our office and design team.
