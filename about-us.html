Our story

Full-service remodeling, personal service.

Top Level Renovation, Inc. is a full-service remodeling, renovation and repair company that has become a go-to in Monmouth County for high-quality craftsmanship, whatever the size of the job.

As a family-run business, we give our clients more personal service. Without the overhead of bigger, less flexible remodeling companies, more of your hard-earned money goes into the work itself.

Every project gets one project manager who coordinates the trades, keeps you updated, usually by group text, and makes sure the job site is left clean at the end of every day. It's the thing our clients mention most in their reviews.
